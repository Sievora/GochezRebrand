Who We Are

Building Nigeria's Trading & Supply Backbone
Since 2012

GOCHEZ RESTO Nigeria Limited was incorporated in Abuja, Nigeria under Registration Number 1088053 on 31 December 2012 — over a decade of adding value to our customers, stakeholders, and the Nigerian economy.

RC 1088053 Registered in Abuja, FCT — 31 December 2012

We offer a comprehensive range of services designed to make trading easy by driving bold ideas and pragmatic solutions. We carry on the business of merchants, traders, importers, exporters, suppliers, manufacturers, makers and producers of products and commodities for industrial, commercial, office, domestic, and other use — whether raw materials, semi-finished, or finished goods.

Beyond energy, we also market, distribute, and manufacture goods including building materials, appliances, furnishings, and electronics, worldwide, both online and offline.

We help your business focus on creating recurring value. Our expertise and knowledge of local and global markets ensure that partnering with us guarantees consistent, compliant, and reliable results.
